# 📣 Announcement

---

**Beginning February 16, 2026, new patient referrals will enter program-based care models** built around what we’ve learned from thousands of real-world cases.

After years of caring for patients alongside hundreds of practitioners, one thing has become clear: better sleep outcomes don’t come from a single test or a “one-size-fits-all” approach. 

Optimal outcomes come from focusing on sleep AND circadian health, clear patient journeys and programs, better clinical validation, and intentional longitudinal care.

Our patients told us they wanted clarity on their care journey and our clinical team knew we could do more with structured assessments and programs.

This evolution allows us to focus on the value we deliver, while giving patients and providers clear endpoints, stronger validation, and better outcomes.

# Overview

---

*We have **three purpose-built programs**, each designed for a different patient need. No subscription anxiety. Just clear goals, defined timelines, and measurable outcomes.*

## Sleep Foundation | *Understand your sleep*

This is the world’s most comprehensive sleep and circadian health assessment. For individuals who want to understand their sleep in-depth, and really get to the root cause. Includes up to 30 nights of sleep testing with a medical grade device, 2 hours of clinician time, analysis of their wearables, and more.

The patient will not walk away with a “prescription.” They will walk away with a root cause analysis on their sleep health + a roadmap on how to get their sleep back on track.

→ [View full program details](Clinical%20Partner%20Resource%20Hub/Sleep%20Foundation%203018328fc76f80eb9784e670b5e9a546.md)

---

## Sleep Optimization | *Assessment + care*

This is for patients who want to understand the root cause of poor sleep and are ready to commit to improving it. This includes continuous sleep testing with synchronous guidance from sleep experts. Includes physician visits, unlimited sleep coaching for respiratory therapy and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia, and full treatment implementation in a defined timeframe.

→ [View full program details](Clinical%20Partner%20Resource%20Hub/Sleep%20Optimization%203028328fc76f80faa0daefed65451b41.md)

---

## Sleep Longevity | *Assessment + care + long-term maintenance*

For individuals who not only want to get their sleep back on track, but those who want optimal sleep year-round. Includes everything in optimization plus a full year of monitoring, coaching, and long-term maintenance.

→ [View full program details](Clinical%20Partner%20Resource%20Hub/Sleep%20Longevity%203028328fc76f80debde5eebcf3c9cf9b.md)
