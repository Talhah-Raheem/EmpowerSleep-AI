# Our Team

Our members are cared for by a focused team of sleep specialists who work together on every case. Here's who's behind the care.

## Sahil Chopra, MD

*Co-Founder & Chief Medical Officer*

Dr. Sahil Chopra is board-certified in Internal Medicine (UCLA), Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine (Loma Linda), and Sleep Medicine (Harvard). His Sleep Medicine fellowship at Harvard under Dr. Robert Thomas fundamentally changed how he thinks about sleep—not as a single diagnosis, but as a physiologic window into someone's total health. 

After fellowship, he couldn't find a practice that treated sleep that way, so he built one. Today he's served thousands of patients globally and his team has assembled one of the largest longitudinal sleep datasets of its kind, powering the precision approach that defines Empower Sleep. 

He's a frequent speaker at leading forums on sleep, longevity, and human performance. Outside of the clinic, he's an avid road cyclist and a dad to three boys (who are doing their best to make sure he never gets a full night's sleep!).

## Stacey Gunn, MD

*Sleep Medicine Physician*

Dr. Stacey Gunn is board certified in Internal Medicine and Sleep Medicine. She completed her medical training at New York University Langone Medical Center (NYULMC) and went on to specialize in sleep medicine at Harvard’s Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center (BIDMC), where she also had the opportunity to stay on as a faculty member. With over a decade in practice, Dr. Gunn proudly embraces her identity as a “sleep nerd,” having been drawn to the field through both personal experience and a deep fascination with the science of sleep, insomnia, breathing disorders during sleep, and circadian rhythms.

What she finds most rewarding about sleep medicine is its potential to be truly life-changing. Poor sleep touches nearly every aspect of health—physical, emotional, and mental—and improving sleep can dramatically shift a patient’s overall well-being. At Empower Sleep, Dr. Gunn values the ability to approach each patient as a whole person, using innovative, data-driven tools to deliver care that is personalized, accessible, and compassionate. She is grateful to be part of a team that prioritizes both empathy and excellence while working to redefine how we understand and treat sleep.

## **Keilah Oberstein-Wright, NP**

*Sleep Coach, Nurse Practitioner*

Keilah is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner. Her clinical background spans urgent care, women's health, medical-surgical nursing, and telemedicine. These experiences taught her to treat patients holistically, not in silos.

She came to sleep medicine the way many of our team members do: through personal experience. After navigating her own sleep struggles and watching the people closest to her go through theirs, she saw how deeply sleep shapes everything else, including energy, mood, recovery, clarity. That's what drives her approach at Empower Sleep: meeting every patient where they are, understanding what their sleep actually looks like, and building a plan that fits their life.

Before joining Empower Sleep, Keilah spent time providing care to underserved communities through medical mission work, delivering basic care, partnering with local churches, and supporting orphanages abroad. That experience shaped her belief that healthcare works best when it starts with listening.

Originally from Florida, Keilah now lives in Colorado with her husband and two daughters. When she's not working with patients, she's usually on a trail or deep in a book.

## **Cali Bahrenfuss, CCSH, RPSGT**

*Sleep Coach, CPAP and Insomnia Therapy*

Cali has spent 20+ years in sleep medicine and has done just about every job in the field—sleep technologist, lab manager, director of operations, regional conference director, remote scoring tech, and insomnia coach. 

Her connection to sleep is also personal: years of night shifts as a traveling tech taught her exactly what sleep deprivation does to a person, and when standard solutions failed her, she found her way to techniques she'd later recognize as the core of CBT-I. She's also 10 years out from a thyroid cancer diagnosis and lives with an autoimmune disorder—and she's open about sharing that story, because it's proof that better sleep is possible even with complex health challenges. 

She joined Empower Sleep because she was tired of institutional red tape getting in the way of doing the right thing for patients. When she's not working, she and her husband are on a mission to visit every National Park in the U.S. (and they're well on their way).

## Laura Avilez, RCP, RRT

*Sleep Coach, CPAP Therapy*

Laura found her way into sleep medicine early in her career as a Respiratory Therapist and has remained deeply committed to it ever since. She's practiced across hospitals, sleep centers, dental sleep medicine, and virtual care—which gives her a perspective on how sleep care works (and doesn't work) across the entire system. 

She joined Empower Sleep because it's the first place she's found that does what she's always believed in: using smart technology to deliver truly personalized care, not hand someone a device and send them on their way. What keeps her going is watching a patient move from chronic sleep problems to actually experiencing quality, restorative sleep.

Outside the clinic, she's a proud mom to a young son and an enthusiastic cook who loves hosting friends and family.
