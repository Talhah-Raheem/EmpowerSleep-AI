# Questions Your Patients May Ask

---

- **Why is it $1,200 for an assessment?**
    
    The Foundation sleep health assessment includes 30 nights of medical-grade testing, 2 hours of clinician time, and a guided roadmap. A traditional in-lab sleep study ranges between $1,000–$7,000 for one night and minimal physician time ([link](https://www.goodrx.com/health-topic/procedures/how-much-sleep-study-cost)). With this assessment, we have more data, more time, and a personalized plan.
    
- **Can you bill insurance for everything?**
    
    Unfortunately, insurances typically only reimburse for single-night testing. We chose to build the program members actually need, not the one insurance will cover. For Optimization and Longevity, we do bill MD and NP visits to insurance where possible, which is why those programs cost less for insured patients.
    
- **I don’t want to sign up for 4 months**
    
    They don't have to. Foundation is one month, no further commitment. If it turns out they need more, they can choose to continue. Nothing is automatic. Nothing is recurring.
    
- **Why can’t I just use my wearable device as a “sleep test?”**
    
    Consumer wearable technology does not capture data with a high enough frequency for a practitioner to diagnose sleep disorders with a high degree of confidence. Plus, they’re not medical-grade. While we integrate with and analyze consumer wearables, they are better to asses trends. They should not be used solely to draw conclusions.
    
- **I already did a sleep test. Should I still join Empower Sleep?**
    
    That's usually why people end up here. A single-night study can miss important aspects of your sleep. It captures one night (often a bad one, in an unfamiliar setting) and draws conclusions from that. Thirty nights of data in your patient's own bed tells a different story.
    
- **I’m already on a sleep treatment like CPAP or a medication. Can Empower Sleep still help?**
    
    Absolutely. This is one of the most common situations we see. A member was previously diagnosed, handed a CPAP or a medication, and left to figure it out on their own. Maybe compliance dropped. Maybe the treatment helped but they're still not sleeping well. Maybe no one ever looked beyond the apnea diagnosis to see what else was going on.
    
    We pick up where that process left off. We'll monitor your current treatment continuously and assess whether it's actually working the way it should. We'll look at what the original workup likely missed: sleep architecture, circadian rhythm, behavioral factors, and how everything fits together.
    
    If your treatment needs adjusting, we adjust it based on actual data. And if what you're already doing is working well, we'll show you the data that proves it and build a plan to keep it that way.
    
- **I've been on sleep medications and want to get off of them. Can Empower Sleep help?**
    
    We hear this a lot. Most of the time, the medication was prescribed without anyone looking at the full picture. Nobody assessed circadian health, sleep architecture, or what was actually causing the problem. The pill treated the symptom. 
    
    We start by figuring out the cause. Continuous testing over weeks shows us what's really going on, and from there we work with you and your prescribing physician on a safe plan to taper. We simultaneously build the behavioral and physiological foundation through CBT-I, circadian rhythm work, and close monitoring so you can sleep without it. We won't promise you'll be off every medication. That depends on what we find. But we'll give you an honest answer and stay with you through the process.
