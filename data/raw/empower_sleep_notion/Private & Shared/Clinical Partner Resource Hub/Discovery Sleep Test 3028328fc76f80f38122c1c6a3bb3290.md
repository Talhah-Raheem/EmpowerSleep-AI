## **Discovery Program Overview**

## Why This Program is Different

- Designed for **in-house screening**, not a referral: your practice manages the patient relationship
- Seamless integration into your clinic workflow—minimal disruption, maximum value.
- Clinically validated testing paired with actionable insights for your team
- Flexible consult options: peer-to-peer with our sleep physician or a joint consult with your patient

---

## Who Should You Screen?

- Patients reporting **sleep complaints**, poor rest, or fatigue.
- Individuals undergoing **other diagnostics** (hormone testing, VO2Max, metabolic panels) where sleep may impact outcome
- High-performance or longevity-focused patients looking to optimize health
- Patients starting **new interventions** (weight management, TRT, training programs, metabolic optimization) where sleep is foundational

---

## How Many Patients Should You Screen?

- Recommendation: ~2 patients per week for a typical clinic workflow. Many longevity and concierge clinics add it as a diagnostic test for all their members
- Adjust based on clinic volume — high-volume practices may screen more
- Goal: create a consistent pipeline to integrate sleep data into overall patient care

    - **Monthly for next 3 months:** monthly check-ins to provide ongoing support to maximize success and answer questions
