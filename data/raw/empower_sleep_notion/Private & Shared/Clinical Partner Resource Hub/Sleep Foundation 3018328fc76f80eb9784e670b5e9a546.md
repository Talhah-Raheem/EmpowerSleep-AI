# This Is Not a “Sleep Apnea Study”

Most sleep evaluations focus on a single question: *“Does my patient have sleep apnea?”*

A traditional sleep study captures one night of data - airflow, oxygen, and basic staging - and typically ends with a binary result or prescription.

Foundation evaluates your patients’ sleep across weeks, not hours. It measures physiology, sleep architecture, timing, behavior, and environment. Rather than screening for a single condition, it maps the full biology to uncover what’s truly driving how your patients feel, perform, and recover.

At the conclusion of the assessment, your patients won’t just have a diagnosis - they’ll have a clear, actionable roadmap.

- **01 — 30 Night At-Home Sleep Test with Multi-Validation**
    
    *Medical-Grade, Real-World Sleep Data*
    
    Sleep changes every night. A single-night sleep study cannot capture this variability — even though these differences are clinically meaningful.
    
    With Foundation, your patients wear an FDA-grade sleep ring for **up to 30 consecutive nights**, capturing continuous, high-resolution data in their natural sleep environment. Each morning, insights are available for review in the provider portal.
    
    Over the testing period, we track night-to-night variability, sleep consistency and regularity, breathing patterns, fragmentation trends, and sleep architecture across the full month. This longitudinal approach allows us to assess sleep apnea, insomnia patterns, and overall sleep stability with a level of precision that one-night studies simply cannot achieve.
    
    We do not rely on a single algorithm to interpret the data. Foundation uses multi-validation testing — applying multiple analytical layers to the same dataset — to increase diagnostic confidence and uncover complex or overlapping sleep conditions that single-night studies routinely miss.
    
    Think of this as a Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) for sleep — providing actionable, real-world insights over time rather than a snapshot.

# Frequently Asked Questions

- **How is this different from a regular sleep study?**
    
    A traditional sleep study is typically a single-night screening focused on detecting sleep apnea. Foundation evaluates the entire sleep system, including physiology, architecture, behaviors, and environment. We measure sleep across one month with a home sleep test. We supplement this data with with multiple sleep testing softwares and consumer wearables, interpreted by a board-certified sleep physician.
    
- **Is this covered by insurance?**
    
    The Foundation program is $1,200. It’s possible to get an insurance reimbursement for the MD/NP telemedicine visit. Certain diagnostic components (such as the single-night sleep test and physician consultation) may be eligible for insurance reimbursement depending on your plan. Our care team can help you understand your options and provide a superbill. To ensure price transparency, we tell you the out of pocket cost, upfront.
    
- Can my patients use their HSA/FSA?
    
    Many of our members have successfully submitted their membership for HSA/FSA reimbursement. Your HSA/FSA administrator will need to determine whether it qualifies as a covered expense. You can try to pay using your HSA/FSA debit cards as a payment method.
    
- **How long does the entire assessment take?**
    
    The full process spans approximately 4 weeks, beginning with at-home sleep testing and culminating in a physician strategy session. Every step is designed to fit into your patients’ life. We will guide them along the way.
    
- **What happens after the assessment?**
    
    Your patients will receive a personalized roadmap with clear, prioritized recommendations. Empower Sleep has multiple programs through which they can receive end-to-end sleep care.
    
- **Can my patient do this from home?**
    
    Yes. The entire assessment is designed to be completed remotely. Sleep testing and consultations all take place from the comfort of their home. This ensures we capture sleep in its most natural state.
    
- **My patient already uses a wearable — why do they need sleep testing?**
    
    Consumer wearables provide useful trend data, but they lack the clinical resolution needed for diagnosis. The assessment uses wearable data as one input among many, cross-referencing it with medical-grade diagnostics to give your patients the full picture. In many cases, we find that wearable data tells a very different story than the clinical findings.
    
- **My patient just needs a quick prescription. Is this the right plan?**
    
    No. We will not write a treatment prescription with Foundation. Rather, this is an assessment with a recommendation and roadmap on how to improve sleep holistically. Our philosophy is to not only “diagnose,” but also monitor our members longitudinally and establish strong continuity of care to ensure the best outcomes. If your patient is looking for a prescription with ongoing treatment, we recommend the Optimization or Longevity programs.
