# **This Is Not "Get a CPAP and Good Luck"**

![CleanShot 2026-02-05 at 18.06.20@2x.png](Sleep%20Optimization/CleanShot_2026-02-05_at_18.06.202x.png)

In traditional sleep medicine, treatment is often one-size-fits-all with single-night testing. “Sleep apnea?” Here's a CPAP. “Insomnia?” Here's a sedative.

We don't practice that way. Every patient's care plan is built around their specific physiology—what we call endotype-driven care. A patient with positional obstructive sleep apnea gets a different plan than someone with central apnea. A patient with sleep-onset insomnia gets a different approach than someone with sleep-maintenance issues driven by circadian misalignment.

We test continuously, so we see how every intervention lands—multiple nights per week, multiple weeks per month. If something isn't working, we adjust. If something is working, we build on it. The data tells us what to do next. Not a protocol. Not a guideline written for the average patient.

It’s like a CGM, but for your sleep.

- **02 — Wearable Data, Clinically Interpreted**
    
    We integrate and analyze your patient's existing devices, including Oura, Apple Watch, WHOOP, Garmin, and Eight Sleep. This gives us access to months of historical sleep and recovery data.
    
    We then validate wearable insights against clinical-grade findings, identify gaps between perceived and physiological sleep, understand how their sleep responds to stress, travel, and lifestyle shifts, and personalize recommendations using longitudinal, real-world data.
    
- **03 — Ongoing Sleep Coach Consultations (Virtual)**
    
    This is the centerpiece of the program.
    
    Data alone, regardless of how accurate it is, does not provide context.
    
    Your patient's sleep coach will spend dedicated time reviewing their medical history, medications, daily routines, stress load, travel patterns, screen habits, and anything else that shapes how they sleep.
    
    This is where we connect their life to their data—identifying the modifiable factors that clinical testing alone can't reveal. In the Optimization program, these check-ins happen throughout the four months so we can track progress, adjust recommendations, and respond to what's actually changing.
    
- **04 — Ongoing Physician Consultations (Virtual)**
    
    Your patient meets one-on-one with a board-certified sleep physician to review every finding throughout their program. Together, they'll explore how their sleep systems interact, identify diagnoses and priorities, and build a personalized, step-by-step optimization plan.
    
    In the Optimization program, physician consultations continue throughout the four months — not just at intake. This allows us to adjust treatment in real time, respond to testing results as they come in, and refine the care plan as your patient progresses.
    
    Physicians can also write prescriptions for specific treatments, all while our coordination team will quarterback the care across DMEs, pharmacies, or any other third-party.
    
    *If you work with a concierge or longevity physician, they're welcome to join these calls. We collaborate with care teams across the country.*
    
- **06 — Unlimited Texting**
    
    Sleep doesn't follow a schedule, and questions come up between appointments. Your patient has direct text access to their care team throughout the program.
    
    Whether it's a question about a new symptom, a travel schedule change, a medication timing issue, or just needing reassurance that what they're experiencing is normal—they can reach us. No portal. No ticket. No waiting for the next appointment.
    
    This keeps care continuous and responsive, which is how real progress happens.
    
- **07 — Care Concierge & Coordination**
    
    Sleep rarely exists in isolation. Your patient may need a dental referral for an oral appliance, coordination with a cardiologist, a letter for their insurance, or help navigating a DME provider for CPAP equipment.
    
    Our care concierge handles all of it. We coordinate across your patient's care team—including your practice—so nothing falls through the cracks. Referrals, records, scheduling, and follow-ups are managed on their behalf.
    
    This is especially valuable for patients with complex, multi-provider care plans. We keep everyone on the same page so your patient doesn't have to.
    
- **05 — Full Treatment Implementation**
    
    This is what separates Optimization from a “sleep apnea test.” We don't hand your patient a report and wish them well. We build the plan, execute it together, and optimize for their physiology.
    
    Based on findings from the initial assessment period, our team implements a full, individualized treatment protocol. That can include medical devices, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I), positional therapy, breathing interventions, light exposure timing, sleep schedule restructuring, supplement guidance, medications, and more.
    
    Every intervention is tracked. Every response is measured. If something isn't working, we adjust. Your patient isn't left guessing whether a recommendation is making a difference—we make data-driven adjustments.
    
- **08 — Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia**
    
    CBT-I is the gold-standard, first-line treatment for chronic insomnia, recommended by the AASM before medication. It works by restructuring the thoughts, habits, and behaviors that keep your patient from sleeping.
    
    Our program is led by experts in CBT-I who trained in the behavioral drivers of poor sleep. Sessions are one-on-one, structured, evidence-based, and personalized—not a generic module or app-based workaround.
    
    We address sleep drive, stimulus control, cognitive restructuring, relaxation training, and sleep restriction when appropriate. For patients whose insomnia sits alongside anxiety, chronic pain, or circadian disruption, we integrate those factors into treatment rather than treating them separately.
    
- **09 — CPAP Therapy and Monitoring**
    
    If your patient is prescribed CPAP, we don't just write the script. We walk them through setup, mask fitting, pressure adjustments, and the early weeks of acclimation—which is where most patients give up. As a result, our CPAP compliance is north of 80% (compared to industry average of 40%-50%).
    
    We monitor compliance data remotely and check in regularly. If the mask is leaking, the pressure feels wrong, or they're pulling it off at 3 a.m., our technology flags it to our practitioners, who troubleshoot before frustration turns into abandonment.
    
    For patients who've tried CPAP before and stopped, we reassess what went wrong and come up with a better solution using better data and insights.
    
- **10 — Oral Appliance Guidance**
    
    For patients with mild to moderate sleep apnea, or those who cannot tolerate CPAP, a mandibular advancement device (oral appliance) can be an effective alternative.
    
    We guide your patient through the evaluation process, help identify qualified sleep dentists in their area, and coordinate the clinical documentation needed to move forward. Once the device is in place, we monitor its impact on their sleep data to confirm it's working.
    
    If your patient is weighing CPAP vs. oral appliance, we help them make that decision based on their specific anatomy, severity, and preferences.
    
- **11 — Alternative Therapies**
    
    One of the unique strengths of our program is the ability for patients to explore non-traditional approaches in a measurable, clinically guided way. Using nightly data, we can monitor the impact of interventions that other sleep clinics often can’t validate.
    
    Examples include lip tape, nasal dilators, chin straps, positional adjustments, and targeted supplements. Patients can test these approaches safely and systematically, allowing us to track what truly improves their sleep and refine recommendations accordingly.
    
    This approach gives patients the flexibility to personalize their care while keeping all adjustments grounded in real-world, physiological feedback.

# Frequently Asked Questions

- **How is this different from a regular sleep study?**
    
    A traditional sleep study is typically a single-night screening focused on detecting sleep apnea. With Optimization, we evaluate your entire sleep system, including physiology, architecture, behaviors, and environment. We measure your sleep across four months with a home sleep testing device. We supplement this data with with multiple sleep testing softwares and consumer wearables, interpreted by a board-certified sleep physician.
    
- **Is this covered by insurance?**
    
    The Sleep Optimization program is $4,000 without insurance and $3,000 with insurance. When using insurance, the MD and NP telemedicine visits will be billed to insurance separately. The non-insurance price includes all of your telemedicine consultations.
    
    Our care team can help you understand your options and verify your benefits prior to you signing up. 
    
    Our goal is to be transparent with pricing, all while providing the most personalized sleep care in a way that’s accessible to you.
    
- **How long does the entire program take?**
    
    The full process spans approximately 4 months, beginning with your at-home sleep testing, designing a roadmap, and then optimizing overtime. Some patients improve their sleep within 2 months, while others may take longer. Everyone’s sleep, diagnosis, and complexity varies, which is why we prefer to have at least four months to collaborate with our members.
    
    Every step is designed to fit into your life. We will guide you along the way.
    
- **Can my patient do this from home?**
    
    Yes. The entire program is designed to be completed remotely. Sleep testing and consultations all take place from the comfort of your home. This ensured we capture your sleep in its most natural state.
    
- **My patient already uses a wearable — why do they need sleep testing?**
    
    Consumer wearables provide useful trend data, but they lack the clinical resolution needed for diagnosis. We uses your patient’s wearable data as one input among many, cross-referencing it with medical-grade diagnostics to understand the full picture. In many cases, we find that wearable data tells a very different story than the clinical findings.
    
- **My patient needs a prescription. Is this the right plan?**
    
    Yes we will prescribe different therapies with the Optimization plan. After your patients starts using a therapy, we will conduct post-therapy sleep testing to measure efficacy. Medical devices like CPAP will be shipped directly to your patient. If we recommend an oral appliance or other medical devices, we will coordinate the care with a specialist nearby.
    
- **What conditions can be treated during Optimization?**
    
    Obstructive and central sleep apnea, chronic insomnia, circadian rhythm disorders, CPAP intolerance, restless leg syndrome, periodic limb movement disorder, narcolepsy, idiopathic hypersomnia, sleep fragmentation, and behavioral sleep issues. 
    
    For patients with overlapping conditions—which is most of them—we treat the full picture, not one diagnosis at a time.
    
- **Is CBT-I included for every patient, or only if needed?**
    
    CBT-I is available to every Optimization patient, but it's not mandatory. If insomnia isn't part of the clinical picture, we focus time and resources elsewhere. Our psychologist assesses each patient individually and recommends CBT-I when it's appropriate.
    
- **How involved do I need to be as the referring physician?**
    
    As much or as little as you'd like. Some physicians join consultations and co-manage care. Others prefer to refer and receive updates. You have access to a partner portal where you can view all of your patient’s data and clinical notes. We send clinical summaries and are always available to discuss your patient's progress. We work with your practice, not around it.
    
- **What if my patient has already tried CPAP and stopped?**
    
    That's common, and it's one of the reasons this program exists. We reassess what went wrong—whether it was pressure settings, mask fit, timing, or tolerance—and build a supported plan to try again with better data and closer monitoring. If CPAP still isn't the right fit, we explore alternatives like oral appliances or positional therapy.
    
    CPAP will typically treat the airway and breathing issue during sleep, but it does not treat sleep holistically. This is why taking a multi-disciplinary approach is critical to optimize for healthy outcomes.
