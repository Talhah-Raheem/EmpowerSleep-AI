## Program Overview & Differentiation

<aside>

### Q. **What is changing?**

---

We’re updating the Empower Sleep program structure and pricing for new patient referrals, launching February 16, 2026. The goal is to improve outcomes for patients, streamline provider workflows, and provide a high-value, predictable care experience. Key enhancements include:

- Structured programs with clear start and end points for measurable progress
- Multi-layered sleep testing and validation for confident clinical decision-making
- Consistent follow-ups and care coordination to improve adherence
- AI-driven insights and coaching to support both patients and providers
- Simplified logistics, including allowing patients to keep their sleep testing rings

These updates ensure every patient receives the right care at the right time, and providers have the tools needed to deliver better outcomes efficiently.

</aside>

<aside>

### Q. **Why is this change happening now?**

---

Open-ended subscriptions and one-off tests can create uncertainty, limit follow-through, and reduce clinical impact. The updated program-based structure addresses these challenges by:

- Providing outcome-driven programs with clear clinical endpoints
- Strengthening clinical decision-making through validated testing
- Supporting continuity of care over time
- Incorporating AI-driven insights and coaching
- Reducing administrative burden for providers

The goal is to deliver measurable improvements in patient health while making the referral and care process predictable and efficient.

</aside>

<aside>

### Q. **Who does this change apply to?**

---

These updates affect new patient referrals starting February 16, 2026. Patients currently in testing, ongoing care, or follow-ups are not impacted.

</aside>

## Clinical Scope & Treatment

<aside>

### Q. **What conditions can be treated?**

---

We address a full range of sleep issues, including:

- Obstructive and central sleep apnea
- Chronic insomnia
- Circadian rhythm disorders
- Restless leg syndrome
- Periodic limb movement disorder
- Narcolepsy
- Idiopathic Insomnia
- CPAP intolerance
- Sleep fragmentation and breathing-related disruptions
- Behavioral sleep issues

Most patients have overlapping conditions, which we treat holistically rather than one diagnosis at a time.

</aside>

<aside>

### Q. **My patient needs a prescription. Which program do you recommend?**

---

Both Optimization and Longevity programs include prescribing and managing appropriate therapies. Post-therapy sleep testing ensures effectiveness. Medical devices (CPAP, oral appliances, etc.) are coordinated and shipped as needed.

</aside>

<aside>

### Q. **What if my patient has tried CPAP before and stopped?**

---

We reassess prior attempts, identify challenges (mask fit, pressure, timing, tolerance), and develop a supported plan with closer monitoring. Alternative treatments, like oral appliances or positional therapy, are explored if CPAP is not suitable.

</aside>

<aside>

### Q. **Is CBT-I included for every patient?**

---

CBT-I is available to all patients when clinically appropriate. The physician evaluates each patient individually and recommends CBT-I only if insomnia is a significant contributor.

</aside>

## Data, Testing, & Wearables

<aside>

### Q. **How is this different from a regular sleep study?**

---

Traditional sleep studies are usually single-night screenings for sleep apnea. Empower Sleep evaluates the entire sleep system—including physiology, circadian health, architecture, behaviors, and environment—over weeks or months with home sleep testing devices, multi-layered validation, and integration of wearable data. All results are interpreted by board-certified sleep physicians.

</aside>

<aside>

### Q. **My patient already uses a wearable. Why do they need sleep testing?**

---

Wearables provide trend data but lack the resolution for clinical decision-making. We use wearable data as one input, cross-referenced with clinical-grade testing, to give a complete and accurate picture.

</aside>

<aside>

### Q. **How often is sleep testing performed?**

---

Testing occurs at multiple points throughout the program to establish baseline, measure treatment response, and guide adjustments.

</aside>

## Pricing, Insurance, and Referrals
