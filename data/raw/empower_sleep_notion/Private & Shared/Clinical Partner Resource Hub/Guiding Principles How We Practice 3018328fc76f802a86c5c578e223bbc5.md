# How We Think About Sleep Care

---

Traditional sleep medicine has a problem. Somewhere along the way, the field narrowed itself down to one question: do you have sleep apnea? A sleep study, in a lab, became a “sleep apnea test.”

An entire discipline built around understanding human physiology during sleep got reduced to a single-night screening tool and a CPAP prescription or medication.

We don't believe in practicing sleep medicine that way.

There are guiding principles behind every clinical decision at Empower Sleep, across every provider.

## **1) A sleep test is not a “sleep apnea test”**

---

A chest X-ray is not a pneumonia test. It's a radiographic visualization of the heart, vasculature, lungs, bony structures, diaphragm. A chest X-ray can reveal dozens of findings beyond pneumonia. 

Similarly, a sleep test is not a “sleep apnea test.”

A sleep study is a measurement of your physiology during sleep: respiratory, cardiovascular, neurological, circadian. It can surface cardiac arrhythmias, circadian rhythm disorders, respiratory conditions, movement disorders, and hormonal imbalances. Apnea is one possible finding. It is not the only one, and it's often not the most important one.

When we do a sleep test, we're not screening for one condition. We're assessing the full physiology of their sleep.

## 2) Sleep should be measured longitudinally

---

In traditional sleep medicine, testing is a one night procedure. Sometimes two, in a lab, or at home, with one algorithm reading the data. There’s a historical reason for that. Historically, sleep testing technology used to be bulky, expensive equipment that required a lab and a technician. Insurance built its codes around that model. When home sleep tests came along, the same single-night framework carried over. The technology has gotten smaller and smarter, but the reimbursement structure and the standard of care haven't caught up.

We test across weeks to months. Continuously. In your patient's own bed.

Longitudinal testing lets us see what a single night never can—night-to-night variability, the effect of interventions over time, circadian patterns that only emerge across weeks, and treatment response tracked in real time. Our patients collect data multiple nights per week. We analyze it across 30, 90, even 365 days. 

Some of our longest-enrolled patients have close to a thousand nights of data.

## 3) We treat the member, not the guideline

---

Most of sleep medicine runs on simple algorithms. Sleep apnea equals autoCPAP. Insomnia equals a sedative or CBT-I. Guideline-based care is applied uniformly across every member regardless of their individual physiology.

We don't practice that way. Every member at Empower Sleep is treated as an N-of-1.

Because we have continuous data, we can see how each member’s sleep responds to specific interventions, including positional changes, medications, CPAP pressure adjustments, behavioral modifications, circadian timing shifts. We can test, measure, adjust, and retest. We can prove what's working and drop what isn't. 

This is endotype-driven care—treatment based on each members underlying physiology, not a population average.

## 4) Sleep without circadian biology is incomplete

---

Sleep is regulated by circadian rhythm. It’s not the other way around.

Circadian timing drives sleep quality, metabolic function, cognitive performance, and recovery. If you're not measuring it, you're missing a fundamental layer of what's actually going on. We incorporate circadian biology into every assessment and every treatment plan. 

It's not a separate service. It's how we practice.

## 5) The care team moves together

---

Your patient doesn't navigate the system alone. 

Our physicians, sleep coaches, and care coordinators work as one team, sharing context, tracking progress, and closing the loop on every order, every referral, every follow-up. Our members have care coordinates to ensure a seamless journey.
We also don't practice in a vacuum. Our clinical team is in continuous dialogue with sleep researchers and specialists at leading academic institutions. When we encounter a complex case, we have direct access to some of the top minds in sleep medicine. That feedback loop—real members, real data, real collaboration with experts—is how our care pathways keep getting sharper. 

Every complex case we see makes the next one better.

## 6) Follow the physiology, not the diagnosis

Insurance categories don't define what an individual needs. We let the data tell us what's happening and treat accordingly, even when it doesn't fit neatly into a billing code. 

The care comes first. We figure out the rest.
