# Referral Process

**Overview:**

Our referral process is designed to be simple, transparent, and supportive for both providers and patients. Below is a step-by-step guide to submitting referrals, what happens after, and how providers stay informed.

---

## Submitting a Referral

**Options for referral:**

- **Provider portal:** You can refer patients directly in your provider portal
- **Direct link:** Use this referral link if you don’t have portal access: [https://provider.empowersleep.com/forms/referral](https://provider.empowersleep.com/forms/referral)
- **Team access:** If a member of your team needs access to the provider portal, contact Steven Strickler at steven@empowersleep.com

**Timeline:**

1. Submit referral → our care team will reach out to the patient within 24-48 hours

---

## Different Patient Intake & Scheduling

**After you refer a patient:**

- Our care team contacts the patient to schedule their initial visit with one of our sleep experts
- We make multiple attempts to reach the patient over 2 weeks after submitting a referral
- We will keep you and your team informed with all of our patient touch points

**First Consultation:**

- Your patient can schedule a complimentary consultation with one of our sleep experts and discuss which program is best

---

## Practitioner Involvement

**Peer-to-Peer Consults:**

- Referring providers can schedule a consult with the physician anytime via the provider portal
- Providers can join patient consults if desired
- We email the referring provider the appointment details and Zoom link

**Access to Patient Data:**

- Clinical chart notes, sleep device data, and sleep reports delivered directly in the portal
- Providers notified of all key milestones and consults

**Customizable involvement:**

- Join consults, review reports, co-manage care, or stay updated passively — your choice
