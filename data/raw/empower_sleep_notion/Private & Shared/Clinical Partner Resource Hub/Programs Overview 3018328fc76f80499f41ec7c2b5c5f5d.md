# Frequently Asked Questions

---

- **Which program should I recommend for my patient?**
    
    If your patient wants clarity on what's going on with their sleep, start with the Foundation assessment. 
    
    If they already know something is wrong and want it fixed, Optimization is the right fit. 
    
    If they're the type of person who wants long-term support and maintenance—executives who travel and experience continuous jet lag, individuals on weight-loss journeys, and others who invest in longevity, performance, or proactive health—Longevity is built for them.
    
- **Can a patient start with Foundation and upgrade later?**
    
    Members can start with Foundation and move into Optimization or Longevity once they see their results. Foundation is designed to stand alone, but it's also the entry point for everything else. These will be billed separately.
    
- **What happens when a program ends?**
    
    The member keeps their sleep testing ring and their data. If they were in Optimization or Longevity, they'll have a clear maintenance plan. They can also re-enroll or step into a longer program if they want continued support.
    
- **Do you replace the referring physician?**
    
    No. We work alongside you. Your patient stays your patient. We handle the sleep-specific workup, treatment, and monitoring, and we keep you in the loop with reports and updates. Think of us as your in-house sleep experts, not a replacement for the relationship you've built.
    
- **What does "multi-validation layer" testing mean?**
    
    There is no one “perfect” sleep test. They all measure sleep with a different perspective. Single-validation testing uses one sleep testing technology to interpret the sleep data. 
    
    Multi-validation means we run the same sleep testing data through multiple softwares that have different algorithms and are medical-grade. This gives us higher confidence in findings and catching things a single pass might miss.
    
- **Can my patients use insurance?**
    
    For Optimization and Longevity, the telemedicine consultations with physicians and NPs can be billed to insurance. Copays and deductibles apply. 
    
    For the Foundation assessment, we can provide a superbill patients can submit to their insurer for potential reimbursement. We do this to expedite time to care.
    
- **Can my patients use their HSA/FSA?**
    
    Many of our members have successfully submitted their membership for HSA/FSA reimbursement. Your HSA/FSA administrator will need to determine whether it qualifies as a covered expense. You can try to pay using your HSA/FSA debit cards as a payment method.
    
- **How do I refer a patient?**
    
    You can refer a patient electronically [here](https://www.empowersleep.com/referral). You can also fax the referral to **(951) 463‑4200.**
    
- **Can I implement Sleep Foundation as a diagnostic test in my office?**
    
    Yes. We have a product called Discovery sleep tests built specifically for this. 
    
    Many concierge and longevity clinics use Discovery to offer sleep testing as an in-house diagnostic without building out the infrastructure themselves. Each Discovery test includes a 30-night medical-grade sleep study for your patient, an MD interpretation, all logistics handled by our team, and a peer-to-peer MD-to-MD consult to review the findings together. It's an affordable way for your patients to get a comprehensive sleep assessment before deciding whether to move into Optimization or Longevity for treatment. Learn more [here](Discovery%20Sleep%20Test%203028328fc76f80f38122c1c6a3bb3290.md) 
    
- **I'm already using Discovery Sleep Tests in my office. Has that program changed?**
    
    No. Everything with Discovery remains the same as we've previously communicated. No changes to pricing, process, or deliverables.
    
- **Can my patient speak with someone before signing up?**
    
    Absolutely. They can schedule a complimentary coaching call with one of our sleep coaches directly on the website. Or, once you refer them, we'll reach out to give them an overview of the program, answer any questions, and make sure it's the right fit.

    No. In the near future, we plan to incorporate more advanced sleep tests, circadian testing, and/or labs into Optimization. This will give our practitioners enough information to form personalized clinical judgements and drive the best outcomes.
